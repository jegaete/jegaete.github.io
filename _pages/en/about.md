<h2>Research interests</h2>

<p>His research is organised around two main lines. The first is <strong>epidemiological</strong>: identifying risk and protective factors for suicidality, depression, self-harm, school violence and substance use among children, adolescents and young adults. The second is the <strong>evaluation of interventions</strong>: testing the efficacy and effectiveness of universal, selective and indicated prevention programmes that address these problems, delivered mainly in school and university settings, including interventions based on cognitive-behavioural therapy and school mental health programmes.</p>

<p>Across both lines, his work seeks to understand the <strong>heterogeneity of symptom trajectories</strong> over time, the role of psychological variables such as psychological inflexibility and sense of belonging, and the social and environmental determinants of young people's mental wellbeing.</p>
